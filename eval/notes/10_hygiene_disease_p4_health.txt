 TOPIC: PERSONAL HYGIENE AND DISEASE PREVENTION

1. MEANING OF PERSONAL HYGIENE
------------------------------
Personal hygiene is the practice of keeping our bodies and our clothes clean
in order to stay healthy.
A germ is a very small living thing that causes disease and cannot be seen
with the naked eye.

2. WAYS OF KEEPING THE BODY CLEAN
---------------------------------
Bathing is the washing of the whole body with clean water and soap.
We should bathe at least twice every day, in the morning and in the evening.

Other practices of personal hygiene:
- Washing hands with soap after visiting the latrine
- Washing hands with soap before eating any food
- Brushing the teeth every morning and before going to bed
- Cutting the finger nails short every week
- Combing the hair and keeping it clean
- Wearing clean clothes and washing them regularly

3. IMPORTANCE OF PERSONAL HYGIENE
---------------------------------
Personal hygiene prevents diseases such as scabies, ringworm and diarrhoea.
It makes us look smart and gives us confidence among other people.
It prevents bad body smell which makes other people uncomfortable.

4. COMMON DISEASES IN OUR COMMUNITY
-----------------------------------
Malaria is a disease that is caused by a parasite and spread by the female
anopheles mosquito.
The signs of malaria are high fever, headache, joint pain and vomiting.
Malaria is prevented by sleeping under a treated mosquito net and by draining
stagnant water around the home.

Cholera is a disease that is caused by drinking water that contains germs.
The signs of cholera are watery diarrhoea, vomiting and severe loss of water
from the body.

Ringworm is a skin disease that is caused by a fungus and it forms round patches.
Scabies is a skin disease that is caused by a small insect that burrows into the skin.

Tuberculosis is a disease of the lungs that spreads through the air when an
infected person coughs.

5. WAYS OF PREVENTING DISEASES
------------------------------
Rules for preventing diseases in the home:
Boil all drinking water before you drink it.
Cover all food to keep away flies that carry germs.
Use a latrine and keep it clean at all times.
Slash the tall grass around the house to remove mosquito breeding places.
Take all children for immunisation at the nearest health centre.

Immunisation is the giving of a vaccine to protect a person against a disease.

6. THE SIX KILLER DISEASES
--------------------------
Children in Uganda are immunised against six killer diseases.
These are measles, whooping cough, tetanus, polio, tuberculosis and diphtheria.
