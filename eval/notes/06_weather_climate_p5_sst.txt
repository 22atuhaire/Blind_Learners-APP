1. MEANING OF WEATHER AND CLIMATE
---------------------------------
Weather is the condition of the atmosphere of a place at a particular time.
Climate is the average weather condition of a place recorded over a long time.

The main difference is that weather changes from day to day while climate
stays the same for many years.

2. ELEMENTS OF WEATHER
----------------------
The elements of weather are the things that make up the weather of a place.
These are rainfall, temperature, humidity, sunshine, wind and cloud cover.

Temperature is the degree of hotness or coldness of a place.
Rainfall is the amount of water that falls from the clouds onto the ground.
Humidity is the amount of water vapour that is present in the air.
Wind is air that is moving from one place to another.

3. INSTRUMENTS FOR MEASURING WEATHER
------------------------------------
A rain gauge is the instrument used for measuring the amount of rainfall.
A thermometer is the instrument used for measuring temperature.
A wind vane is the instrument that shows the direction of the wind.
An anemometer is the instrument used for measuring the speed of the wind.
A hygrometer is the instrument used for measuring humidity in the air.
A barometer is the instrument used for measuring atmospheric pressure.

A weather station is a place where the elements of weather are recorded.
The instruments at a weather station are kept in a Stevenson screen.

4. TYPES OF RAINFALL
--------------------
There are three main types of rainfall.

Relief rainfall is the rainfall that forms when moist air is forced to rise
over a mountain.
Convectional rainfall is the rainfall that forms when the ground heats the air
and the warm air rises.
Frontal rainfall is the rainfall that forms when a mass of warm air meets a
mass of cold air.

Uganda receives mostly convectional rainfall because it lies near the equator.

5. FACTORS AFFECTING CLIMATE
----------------------------
Altitude is the height of a place above sea level.
Places at a high altitude such as Kabale are colder than places at a low altitude.
Latitude is the distance of a place from the equator.
Nearness to a water body makes a place receive more rainfall.
Vegetation cover increases rainfall because trees release water vapour.

6. IMPORTANCE OF WEATHER FORECASTING
------------------------------------
Weather forecasting is the prediction of the weather of a place in advance.
Farmers use weather forecasts to know when to plant their crops.
Fishermen use weather forecasts to know when it is safe to go onto the lake.
Pilots use weather forecasts to plan safe flights.
