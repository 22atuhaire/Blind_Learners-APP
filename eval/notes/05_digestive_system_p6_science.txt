1. INTRODUCTION
---------------
Digestion is the process by which food is broken down into small particles
that the body can absorb and use.
The digestive system is the group of organs that work together to digest food.

2. THE ALIMENTARY CANAL
-----------------------
The alimentary canal is the long tube that runs from the mouth to the anus.
Food passes through the following parts in order:
mouth, oesophagus, stomach, small intestine, large intestine, rectum and anus.

3. PARTS AND THEIR FUNCTIONS
----------------------------
The mouth is the part where digestion begins.
The teeth are the organs that cut, tear and grind food into smaller pieces.
Saliva is the liquid produced in the mouth that softens food and begins
the digestion of starch.
The tongue is the muscular organ that mixes food with saliva and pushes it down.

The oesophagus is the tube that carries food from the mouth to the stomach.
Peristalsis is the wave-like movement of muscles that pushes food along the gut.

The stomach is the bag-like organ that stores food and mixes it with gastric juice.
Gastric juice is the liquid in the stomach that digests proteins.
Hydrochloric acid is the acid in the stomach that kills germs found in food.

The small intestine is the part where most digestion and absorption take place.
Bile is the green liquid made by the liver that breaks fat into small droplets.
The liver is the largest gland in the body and it produces bile.
The pancreas is the gland that produces pancreatic juice.

The large intestine is the part that absorbs water from undigested food.
The rectum is the part that stores faeces before they are passed out.

4. TYPES OF TEETH
-----------------
Human beings have four types of teeth.

Incisors are the front teeth used for cutting and biting food.
Canines are the pointed teeth used for tearing food.
Premolars are the teeth used for crushing and grinding food.
Molars are the large back teeth used for chewing and grinding food.

An adult person has thirty two permanent teeth.
A child has twenty milk teeth.

5. CARE OF THE TEETH
--------------------
Rules for caring for the teeth:
Brush your teeth at least twice every day, in the morning and before sleeping.
Avoid eating too many sweets because sugar causes tooth decay.
Do not open bottles with your teeth.
Visit a dentist when you feel pain in a tooth.

Tooth decay is the destruction of a tooth caused by germs acting on sugar.

6. DISEASES AND DISORDERS
-------------------------
Constipation is a condition where a person fails to pass out faeces easily.
Diarrhoea is a condition where a person passes watery stool many times a day.
Constipation is prevented by eating foods with roughage and drinking enough water.
