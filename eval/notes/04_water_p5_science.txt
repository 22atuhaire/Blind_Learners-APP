1. WHAT IS WATER?
-----------------
Water is a colourless, tasteless and odourless liquid that has no smell.
Water is life. Every living thing needs water to stay alive.

2. SOURCES OF WATER
-------------------
In Uganda we get water from many different places.

Natural sources of water:
- Rain
- Rivers such as River Nile
- Lakes such as Lake Victoria
- Springs
- Wells
- Swamps

Artificial sources of water:
- Boreholes
- Dams
- Tap water from the National Water and Sewerage Corporation

3. USES OF WATER
----------------
Water is used at home and at school for many activities.
Uses of water at home:
- Drinking and cooking food
- Washing clothes, plates and the house
- Bathing and keeping the body clean
- Watering plants in the garden

Water is also used in industries for making drinks and for cooling machines.

4. STATES OF WATER
------------------
Water exists in three states.
A solid state of water is ice, which forms when water is cooled below zero degrees.
A liquid state of water is the ordinary water we drink from a cup.
A gas state of water is water vapour, which forms when water is heated.

The change from liquid water to water vapour is called evaporation.
The change from water vapour back to liquid water is called condensation.
Melting is the change from ice to liquid water.
Freezing is the change from liquid water to ice.

5. SAFE AND UNSAFE WATER
------------------------
Safe water is water that is free from germs and dirt and is good for drinking.
Unsafe water is water that contains germs, dirt or chemicals that cause disease.

Signs of unsafe water:
- It looks dirty or has a brown colour
- It has a bad smell
- It has small living things swimming inside

6. METHODS OF MAKING WATER SAFE
-------------------------------
Boiling is the commonest method of making water safe at home.
Filtering removes solid dirt from water by passing it through a clean cloth or filter.
Adding water guard or chlorine kills the germs that are found in water.
Sedimentation is the process of letting dirt settle at the bottom of a container.

Example: A family in Gulu draws water from an open well.
The water looks brown, so the mother first filters it through a clean cloth.
She then boils it for ten minutes and stores it in a covered pot.

7. WATER BORNE DISEASES
-----------------------
Water borne diseases are diseases that people get from drinking unsafe water.
Cholera is a water borne disease that causes severe diarrhoea and vomiting.
Typhoid is a water borne disease that causes high fever and stomach pain.
Dysentery is a disease that causes diarrhoea with blood in the stool.

Prevention of water borne diseases:
- Always drink boiled or treated water
- Wash hands with soap before eating
- Keep latrines far away from water sources
- Cover all stored water
