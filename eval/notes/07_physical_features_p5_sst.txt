1. MEANING OF PHYSICAL FEATURES
-------------------------------
Physical features are the natural things that are found on the surface of the earth.
They include mountains, lakes, rivers, valleys, plateaus and plains.

2. MOUNTAINS OF UGANDA
----------------------
A mountain is a landform that rises much higher than the land around it.

Mount Rwenzori is the highest mountain in Uganda and it is found in the west.
The highest peak of Mount Rwenzori is called Margherita.
Mount Elgon is an extinct volcano that is found in the east of Uganda.
Mount Moroto is a mountain found in the north east of Uganda in Karamoja.

Block mountains are mountains that form when land between two faults is pushed up.
Volcanic mountains are mountains that form when molten rock comes out of the earth.
Mount Rwenzori is a block mountain while Mount Elgon is a volcanic mountain.

Importance of mountains:
- They attract tourists who bring foreign exchange
- They cause relief rainfall on their windward side
- They have fertile volcanic soils good for growing crops
- They are sources of rivers

3. LAKES OF UGANDA
------------------
A lake is a large body of water that is surrounded by land.

Lake Victoria is the largest lake in Uganda and in the whole of Africa.
Lake Albert is a rift valley lake found in the western part of Uganda.
Lake Kyoga is a shallow lake found in the central part of Uganda.
Lake Bunyonyi is the deepest lake in Uganda.

Crater lakes are small round lakes that form in the mouth of an old volcano.

4. RIVERS OF UGANDA
-------------------
A river is a natural stream of water flowing towards a lake, a sea or an ocean.

River Nile is the longest river in Africa and it starts from Lake Victoria at Jinja.
River Katonga is a river that flows into Lake Victoria.
River Kagera forms part of the border between Uganda and Tanzania.

Importance of rivers:
- They provide water for domestic and industrial use
- They are used for transport by boats
- They are used for generating hydro electricity at Owen Falls
- They provide fish which is a source of food and income

5. THE RIFT VALLEY
------------------
A rift valley is a long deep valley that forms when land sinks between two faults.
The western arm of the East African rift valley passes through western Uganda.
Lake Albert, Lake Edward and Lake George are found in the western rift valley.
