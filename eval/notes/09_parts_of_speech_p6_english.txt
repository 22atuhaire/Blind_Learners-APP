ENGLISH NOTES FOR PRIMARY SIX (P.6)

TOPIC: PARTS OF SPEECH

1. INTRODUCTION

A part of speech is the name given to a word according to the work it does
in a sentence. There are eight parts of speech in the English language.

2. NOUNS

A noun is a word that names a person, a place, an animal or a thing.
A common noun is a noun that names any one of a group of things, such as boy or town.
A proper noun is a noun that names a particular person or place and begins
with a capital letter, such as Joseph or Kampala.
A collective noun is a noun that names a group of things taken together,
such as a herd of cattle.
An abstract noun is a noun that names something we can think of but cannot touch,
such as honesty or happiness.

3. PRONOUNS

A pronoun is a word that is used in place of a noun.
Examples of pronouns are I, you, he, she, it, we and they.

Example: Mary is my sister. She is a nurse.
In the second sentence, the pronoun she is used in place of the noun Mary.

4. VERBS

A verb is a word that shows an action or a state of being.
An action verb is a verb that shows what somebody or something does, such as run.
A helping verb is a verb that is used together with a main verb, such as is or have.

5. ADJECTIVES

An adjective is a word that describes a noun or a pronoun.
Examples of adjectives are tall, red, clever, heavy and beautiful.

Example: The tall boy carried a heavy box.
The adjective tall describes the noun boy.

6. ADVERBS

An adverb is a word that describes a verb, an adjective or another adverb.
Many adverbs end with the letters ly, such as quickly, slowly and carefully.

Example: The girl walked slowly to school.
The adverb slowly describes the verb walked.

7. PREPOSITIONS

A preposition is a word that shows the position or relationship of a noun
to another word in the sentence.
Examples of prepositions are on, in, under, between, behind and near.

8. CONJUNCTIONS

A conjunction is a word that joins words, phrases or sentences together.
Examples of conjunctions are and, but, or, because and although.

9. INTERJECTIONS

An interjection is a word that expresses a sudden strong feeling.
Examples of interjections are Oh, Alas, Hurrah and Ouch.
