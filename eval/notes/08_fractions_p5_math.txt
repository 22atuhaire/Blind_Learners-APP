MATHEMATICS NOTES FOR PRIMARY FIVE (P.5)

TOPIC: FRACTIONS

1. MEANING OF A FRACTION

A fraction is a number that represents a part of a whole.
Every fraction has two parts, the numerator and the denominator.
The numerator is the number written above the line in a fraction.
The denominator is the number written below the line in a fraction.

Example: In the fraction 3/4, the numerator is 3 and the denominator is 4.

2. TYPES OF FRACTIONS

A proper fraction is a fraction whose numerator is smaller than its denominator.
An improper fraction is a fraction whose numerator is bigger than its denominator.
A mixed number is a number that has a whole number part and a fraction part.
Equivalent fractions are fractions that have the same value but different numbers.

Example: 1/2, 2/4 and 4/8 are equivalent fractions because they all mean a half.

3. CHANGING IMPROPER FRACTIONS TO MIXED NUMBERS

Rules for changing an improper fraction to a mixed number:
Divide the numerator by the denominator.
Write the whole number answer first.
Write the remainder as the new numerator over the same denominator.

Example: Change 7/2 to a mixed number.
Divide 7 by 2 to get 3 remainder 1.
Answer: 3 1/2.

4. ADDITION AND SUBTRACTION OF FRACTIONS

When the denominators are the same, you add or subtract only the numerators.
When the denominators are different, you must first find the lowest common multiple.

Example: Work out 1/4 + 2/4.
The denominators are the same, so add the numerators: 1 + 2 = 3.
Answer: 3/4.

Example: Work out 1/2 + 1/3.
The lowest common multiple of 2 and 3 is 6.
Change the fractions: 1/2 becomes 3/6 and 1/3 becomes 2/6.
Add: 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6.

5. MULTIPLICATION OF FRACTIONS

To multiply fractions you multiply the numerators together and the denominators together.

Example: Work out 2/3 x 3/5.
Multiply the numerators: 2 x 3 = 6.
Multiply the denominators: 3 x 5 = 15.
Answer: 6/15 which simplifies to 2/5.

6. FRACTIONS OF QUANTITIES

To find a fraction of a quantity you multiply the fraction by the quantity.

Example: Find 1/4 of 20 mangoes.
Multiply 1/4 by 20 to get 5.
Answer: 5 mangoes.
