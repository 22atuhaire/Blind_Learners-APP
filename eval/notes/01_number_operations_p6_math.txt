MATHEMATICS NOTES FOR PRIMARY SIX (P.6)

TOPIC: HIGHER NUMBER OPERATIONS

1. PLACE VALUE AND VALUE OF LARGE NUMBERS

In Primary Six, you work with numbers up to 7 digits or more. You must know the place value and the total value of each digit. The place value is the position of a digit in a number (like Ones, Tens, Hundreds, Thousands, Ten Thousands, Hundred Thousands, Millions).

Example: In the number 4,567,890, the digit 5 is in the Hundred Thousands place, so its total value is 500,000.

Example: In the number 3,486,789, if the digit 8 is underlined, its place value is Ten Thousands and its total value is 80,000.

2. READING AND WRITING LARGE NUMBERS

You need to convert figures to words and words to figures.

Example: Write 35,000,900,010 in words.
Break the number into periods: 35 (billions), 000 (millions), 900 (thousands), 010 (units).
Read each period: Thirty-five billion, nine hundred thousand, ten.
Answer: Thirty-five billion, nine hundred thousand, ten.

Example: Write the following in symbols: Five hundred and ninety million, seven hundred thousand, five hundred.
Break into parts: 590,000,000 + 700,000 + 500 = 590,700,500.

3. ROUNDING OFF NUMBERS

You round numbers to the nearest ten, hundred, thousand, or other place value.

Rules for rounding:
Look at the digit to the right of the place you are rounding to.
If that digit is 5 or more, round up.
If that digit is 4 or less, round down.

Example: Round 89,365 to the nearest 100.
Look at the hundreds digit (3) and the digit to the right (6).
Since 6 is 5 or more, round up. 89,365 becomes 89,400.

4. ADDITION AND SUBTRACTION OF LARGE NUMBERS

You add and subtract numbers with up to 7 digits or more. Arrange the numbers correctly according to place values.

Key terms:
Addends are the numbers being added. The answer is the sum.
In subtraction, the number being subtracted from is the minuend, the number subtracted is the subtrahend, and the answer is the difference.

Example 1: Find the sum of 389,754 and 23,657.
Arrange vertically and add.
  389,754
+  23,657
---------
  413,411

Example 2: Find the difference of 678,231 and 354,986.
Arrange vertically and subtract. You may need to borrow.
  678,231
- 354,986
---------
  323,245

Example 3: What must be added to 237,428 to get 885,643?
This is a subtraction problem: 885,643 - 237,428 = 648,215.

Example 4: A dairy processed 5,300,450 litres of milk and sold 3,450,833 litres. How many litres were left?
Subtract: 5,300,450 - 3,450,833 = 1,849,617 litres.

5. MULTIPLICATION OF LARGER NUMBERS

You multiply 3-digit numbers by 2-digit numbers.

Example: Multiply 345 by 27.
  345
x  27
-----
 2415   (345 x 7)
 6900   (345 x 20)
-----
 9315

6. DIVISION OF LARGER NUMBERS

You divide large numbers by 2-digit numbers.

Example: Divide 1,248 by 24.
24 goes into 124 five times (24 x 5 = 120), remainder 4. Bring down 8 to make 48. 24 goes into 48 two times (24 x 2 = 48), remainder 0.
Answer: 52.

7. MIXED OPERATIONS (BODMAS)

When a number sentence has more than one operation, you must follow the order of operations: BODMAS.

BODMAS stands for:
Brackets
Orders (powers and roots)
Division
Multiplication
Addition
Subtraction

Example: Work out 345,783 – 472,096 + 432,562.
Following BODMAS, addition and subtraction are done from left to right.
First, add 345,783 and 432,562 = 778,345.
Then subtract 472,096: 778,345 - 472,096 = 306,249.

Example: Work out (45 + 23) x 6.
First, do the brackets: 45 + 23 = 68.
Then multiply: 68 x 6 = 408.

8. DIVISIBILITY TESTS

You use divisibility tests to check if a number can be divided by another number without a remainder.

Rules for divisibility:
A number is divisible by 2 if its last digit is even (0, 2, 4, 6, 8).
A number is divisible by 3 if the sum of its digits is divisible by 3.
A number is divisible by 5 if its last digit is 0 or 5.
A number is divisible by 6 if it is divisible by both 2 and 3.
A number is divisible by 9 if the sum of its digits is divisible by 9.
A number is divisible by 10 if its last digit is 0.

Example: Is 612 divisible by 6?
Check divisibility by 2: last digit is 2, so yes.
Check divisibility by 3: sum of digits is 6+1+2=9, which is divisible by 3, so yes.
Since 612 is divisible by both 2 and 3, it is divisible by 6.

Example: Is 20,121 divisible by 3?
Sum of digits: 2+0+1+2+1=6, which is divisible by 3, so yes.

9. NUMBER PATTERNS AND SEQUENCES

You identify and complete number patterns.

Example: Find the next two numbers in the pattern: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, ...
This is a pattern of adding 2 each time. The next numbers are 12 and 14.
